The Concept

One arc.
Two natures.

A single arc that begins void
and becomes amber. The form
never changes. Only its nature does.

Void arc = physician speaking.
Amber arc = AI documenting.
Gap = moment of capture.
Dot = clinical note, complete.

Geometry

Golden ratio
split.

Center (80,80) Radius 58

Void arc:
(80,22) → (134,101) CW
61.8% of arc · φ length

Amber arc:
(134,101) → (94,136.3) CW
Stops 14° before terminus

Gap: ~8px arc end to dot edge
Dot: cx=80 cy=138 r=6
Ring: r=13 opacity=0.22

The Name

Inflection:
three meanings.

Linguistics: modification of
voice to express meaning.
Voice inflects to become record.

Mathematics: the point where
a curve changes its nature.

Medicine: where raw data
becomes diagnosis.

All three are Nuvoc.

02 · Mark Anatomy

Three Elements

Void Arc — The Human Voice Begins at 12 o'clock, sweeps clockwise in white. The physician speaking. Travels exactly 61.8% of the arc — the golden ratio — before anything changes.
φ Split — The Inflection Point At 61.8% the arc becomes amber. Not at the midpoint — at the proportion nature uses for growth. The color transition is the entire brand concept in one gesture.
Gap + Dot — The Record The amber arc stops short. A deliberate gap. Then the dot — the clinical note, complete. The breath between arc and dot is the moment of capture.

07 · Usage Rules

Always

  • Use the mark on void, paper, or amber backgrounds only
  • Maintain clear space equal to dot diameter on all sides
  • Use Outfit weight 200 exclusively for the wordmark
  • NU filled amber — VOC transparent with stroke
  • Dark stroke on VOC when mark is on light background
  • Scale proportionally — never stretch or distort
  • Preserve the φ split — geometry is exact
  • Preserve the gap — arc must not touch the dot

Never

  • Never recolor the amber elements to any other color
  • Never rotate or flip — arc always opens to the right
  • Never use below 24px without switching to favicon version
  • Never place on photographic or patterned backgrounds
  • Never use Outfit weights other than 200 for wordmark
  • Never add shadows, glows, outlines, or gradients
  • Never close the gap — arc must stop short of dot
  • Never alter the inflection point position
